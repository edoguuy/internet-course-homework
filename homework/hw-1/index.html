Who I Am

Hi, I'm Edo, a third-year Industrial Engineering and Management student at Ruppin Academic Center.

These experiences gave me a strong background in leadership, teamwork, and solving problems.

Today, I'm interested in combining my management experience with technology, programming, and artificial intelligence.

I created this website to share a little about myself, my professional background, and my experience in the ski industry.

You can learn more about my background on the About Me page and read about my ski experience on the Notes page.
