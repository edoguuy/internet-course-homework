My Ski Journey

From my passion for skiing to managing operations in the French Alps.

Before France

My Passion for Skiing

Skiing has been one of my favorite sports for many years. Even before I started working in France, I already had experience as a skier and really enjoyed spending time in the mountains.

In 2021, I got the opportunity to combine my love for skiing with a new professional experience. I decided to move to France for the winter season and work in the ski tourism industry.

At that time, I didn't know that this opportunity would become such an important part of my professional life.

Working at Pingwin Ski

My First Season in Tignes

2021/22

My first season with Pingwin Ski was in Tignes, France.

I worked as a company representative, helping Israeli tourists during their ski vacations. My responsibilities included helping guests with their hotels, ski passes, transportation, and problems that came up during their stay.

I learned how important customer service is and how to find solutions quickly when things don't go as planned.

It was an amazing experience because I could enjoy the ski environment while learning new professional skills.

Becoming an Operations Manager

2022/23–2023/24

After my first season, I was promoted to Operations Manager for Pingwin Ski in France.

During the next two winter seasons, I managed a team of around 15 representatives working at different ski resorts. Together, we were responsible for approximately 500–600 customers every week.

My responsibilities included coordinating with hotels, transportation companies, ski pass providers, and the company's office. I also made financial and operational decisions in the field and helped my team solve unexpected problems.

This experience taught me a lot about managing people, planning operations, and working under pressure.

Training the Next Teams

Another important part of my work was helping recruit and train new representatives for the company.

I was involved in selecting candidates, preparing them for their roles, and training teams both in Israel and in France.

I enjoyed sharing my experience and helping new representatives understand their responsibilities.

This part of the job showed me that being a manager is not only about making decisions. It is also about helping other people become better at their jobs.

From Military Leadership to the Ski Industry

How My Military Experience Helped Me

2017–2021

Before the ski industry, I served as an officer in the IDF, where I learned to lead teams and make decisions under pressure (more on my About Me page).

When I became an Operations Manager in France, I realized how useful these skills were.

Managing ski vacations is very different from military service, but both require teamwork, planning, leadership, and the ability to solve unexpected problems.

Today, as an Industrial Engineering and Management student, I can see how my previous experience connects to the subjects I study.

Good planning is important, but the ability to lead a team and solve problems when things change is what makes the difference.
