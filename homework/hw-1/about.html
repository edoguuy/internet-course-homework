About Me

Industrial Engineering and Management student with a background in leadership, operations, and a passion for technology.

A Little About Myself

I'm a third-year student at Ruppin Academic Center.

I have experience in team management and operations, and I'm interested in combining it with technology and engineering.

I enjoy solving problems, learning new things, and finding ways to make processes more efficient.

My Military Experience

I served in the IDF from 2017 to 2021 and completed my service with the rank of Captain.

During my service, I worked in different intelligence positions, including as a Research Officer in the Northern Command and later as a Deputy Intelligence Officer in an armored brigade.

These roles gave me valuable experience in leading teams, analyzing information, working with different units, and making important decisions under pressure.

My military service had a big influence on the way I work and manage people today.

My Professional Background

After my military service, I worked in the ski tourism industry in France.

I started as a company representative at Pingwin Ski and later became the Operations Manager for the company's activities in France.

This experience allowed me to develop my management skills in a completely different environment, working with teams, customers, and service providers.

You can read more about this experience on my Notes page.

My Studies and Interest in Technology

During my studies, I've learned programming languages such as Java, Python, and C#, and worked with SQL and other technical tools.

I'm especially interested in information systems, artificial intelligence, and using technology to solve real problems.

Recently, I've also started working on personal AI projects to gain practical experience and improve my technical skills.

My goal is to combine my previous management experience with the technical knowledge I'm gaining during my studies.

Beyond Work and Studies

In 2023, I volunteered in Nepal with a group of Israeli military veterans.

I led a small team that helped renovate a local school and organized educational activities for children.

It was a meaningful experience that taught me a lot about teamwork, responsibility, and working with people from different backgrounds.

In my free time, I enjoy skiing, traveling, and exploring new technologies.
